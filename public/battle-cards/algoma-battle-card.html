Defensible Claim Language

Algoma and BidDeed.AI operate in parallel markets with minimal overlap. Algoma replaces the $28K-$75K architect feasibility study for commercial developers. BidDeed.AI replaces the manual foreclosure research process for distressed-property investors. Both are AI-first. Both serve real estate professionals. But the customer journey is different: BidDeed starts at the auction docket, Algoma starts at the raw land site. Our 12 patent claims protect capabilities Algoma has no reason to build — auction intelligence, bid formula optimization, cross-auction correlation, agentic pipeline orchestration. If Algoma ever pivoted to distressed property, they'd face both our data moat (256,650+ auction records) and our IP moat (12 claims).
