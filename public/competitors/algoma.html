Threat Level: Low

Algoma vs BidDeed.AI

Algoma operates in the feasibility and construction intelligence space — helping developers assess whether a project is buildable and cost-effective. They are a parallel-market player, not a direct competitor. Different customer, different use case, minimal overlap with our foreclosure auction intelligence platform.

Category: Feasibility + Construction

Parallel Market — Not Direct Competition

Algoma and BidDeed.AI serve fundamentally different stages of the real estate lifecycle. Algoma helps developers answer "Can I build this project profitably?" after they've already acquired land. BidDeed.AI helps investors answer "Should I acquire this distressed property?" before the auction. These are complementary workflows, not competing ones. A developer could use BidDeed.AI to acquire a foreclosure property and then use Algoma to assess construction feasibility — both products win.

Competitor Profile
AL

Algoma

Feasibility + Construction Intelligence · Development-stage focus

Algoma provides construction feasibility analysis for real estate developers. Their platform estimates construction costs, assesses site constraints, evaluates building code compliance, and generates pro forma financial models for new development projects. They target commercial and residential developers who need to determine whether a site can support their intended project.

Their strength is construction cost intelligence and feasibility modeling. Their weakness relative to our space: they have zero foreclosure or auction intelligence, no distressed property pipeline, and no ML-driven deal scoring. They operate entirely in the post-acquisition, pre-construction phase — a different market entirely.

Why Algoma Is Not a Threat

Different Customer Segment

Algoma serves developers who already own land and need construction feasibility. BidDeed.AI serves investors seeking distressed acquisition opportunities. Almost zero customer overlap — our users are buying properties, theirs are building on them.

Different Data Domain

Algoma's core data is construction costs, building codes, and site engineering constraints. Our core data is auction records, lien positions, property valuations, and foreclosure proceedings. No data overlap means no competitive pressure on data moats.

No Auction Pipeline

Algoma has zero integration with county clerk systems, no foreclosure data ingestion, and no concept of auction bidding. Building this capability would require a fundamentally different product — essentially building BidDeed.AI from scratch.

Potential Integration Partner

Rather than a competitor, Algoma could be a downstream integration partner. Investors who acquire properties through BidDeed.AI and plan to develop them could use Algoma for feasibility — creating a natural handoff workflow.

The Kill Shot

Algoma and BidDeed.AI don't compete — they complement.
Algoma answers: "Can I build on this site profitably?"
BidDeed.AI answers: "Should I acquire this distressed property?"

These are sequential stages in the real estate lifecycle. Our 12 patent claims protect auction intelligence capabilities that Algoma has no reason to build — and if they ever pivoted into our space, they'd face both our data moat (245K+ auction records) and our IP moat (12 claims covering the full agentic pipeline).

Strategic Verdict

Threat Level: LOW — Algoma operates in a parallel market (feasibility + construction) with minimal overlap to our foreclosure auction intelligence platform. Different customers, different data, different use case.

Recommended Posture: Monitor annually. Explore potential integration partnership — BidDeed.AI acquisition intelligence feeding into Algoma feasibility analysis could create a compelling end-to-end workflow for investor-developers.

Patent Claims Engaged: 8 of 12 protected, 4 parallel-market N/A — Algoma operates in a domain where most of our claims are simply irrelevant, not contested.
